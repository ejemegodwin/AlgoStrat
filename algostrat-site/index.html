Frequently asked questions

What is AlgoStrat?

AlgoStrat is where your work history is provable. You complete small, real tasks against written criteria. Each reviewed task issues a verifiable receipt that proves the work was done.

How do work receipts work?

Each task has explicit criteria. After you submit, a reviewer checks your work against those criteria. If you pass, you receive a receipt with a hash of the canonical task data. You can verify that hash in your browser.

Can beginners participate?

Yes. Tasks are scoped to be achievable by someone starting out. You begin at Level 0 and progress as you earn verified receipts.

Do receipts guarantee a paid job?

No. Receipts demonstrate capability but do not guarantee employment or income. They are one form of evidence among many that you can share.

How do businesses define successful work?

Businesses write the task and its criteria before work begins. Reviewers check submissions against that explicit list — not against a resume or portfolio.

What happens when work does not meet the criteria?

The review identifies which criteria were met and which were not. You receive specific feedback and can resubmit. No work is permanently failed.

Are the level rules final?

No. The current level thresholds are an illustrative draft. Final rules will be published before they take effect.

Is the platform available now?

AlgoStrat is in early development. Join the waitlist to get notified when tasks and reviews begin.
